# 90-day Azerbaijan solar content and authority program

No price, warranty, generation, savings, project, or regional claim should be published until a Volt owner confirms the source data. Azerbaijani is the primary editorial language; Russian receives complete core commercial coverage and versions of subjects that demonstrate demand.

## Month 1

1. **Günəş paneli qiymətləri Azərbaycanda** — explain equipment, capacity, mounting, protection, cable, survey, and commissioning price factors. Add confirmed price ranges only after approval.
2. **5 kW və 10 kW ev sistemi** — use anonymized consumption profiles, roof constraints, expected-production methodology, and confirmed equipment combinations.
3. **On-grid, off-grid və hibrid inverter müqayisəsi** — decision table for grid dependence, batteries, backup, use case, and limitations.
4. **Günəş paneli quraşdırılması** — survey-to-commissioning workflow linked to the Ministry of Energy guidance and State Committee requirements.

## Month 2

1. Payback and electricity-savings methodology using published tariffs and explicitly dated assumptions.
2. Battery and backup sizing based on critical loads, autonomy, usable capacity, and inverter compatibility.
3. Solar systems for farms and irrigation using a genuine load profile and seasonal constraints.
4. One completed residential or commercial case study with customer permission, original photographs, installed capacity, equipment, dates, and measured or clearly modelled results.

## Month 3

1. Maintenance, lifespan, and warranty guide based on the actual products Volt sells and supports.
2. Solar for warehouses, factories, and offices using daytime-load examples.
3. A regional installation case study backed by service capability, project evidence, and photographs.
4. Common buying mistakes: underspecified protection, incompatible strings, shading, unrealistic savings claims, weak service coverage, and warranty ambiguity.

## Required page elements

Each published piece needs an expert Azerbaijani review, named update date, evidence or methodology, useful internal links, one relevant commercial page, one project where available, FAQ content that is visible on the page, and clear quote/WhatsApp/phone actions. Do not mass-create city pages or mechanically translate every article.

## Google Business Profile and outreach

- Keep the legal business name, address, phone, website, opening hours, service areas, and solar categories consistent.
- Publish original project photographs and regular AZ/RU project updates.
- Ask for genuine reviews only after completed work and respond in the customer's language.
- Pursue relevant manufacturer, distributor, engineering-partner, client, association, local-media, and renewable-energy citations.
- Reuse approved installations as website case studies, Google Business Profile posts, videos, and outreach assets.
- Do not buy links, reviews, bulk directory listings, or low-quality guest posts.

## Monthly review

Compare the last 28 and 90 days against their preceding periods. Review non-branded cluster impressions/clicks, strategic-page indexation, genuine leads by source, Core Web Vitals, query-to-page alignment, and AI/search crawler access. Update the next month's briefs from measured demand rather than publishing by volume alone.
